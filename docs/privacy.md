# Privacy and Data Governance Policy

## Principles

1. **Data Minimization**: ProfLearn collects only information necessary to deliver educational experiences: email, name, role, course enrollments, learning progress, and course questions.
2. **Content Ownership**: Course materials uploaded by professors remain the intellectual property of the professor and their institution. Uploaded documents are processed solely for the respective course's lecture generation and RAG retrieval.
3. **No Training on User Data**: Data sent to third-party AI providers is explicitly not used for model training or refinement.
4. **Student Privacy**: Professors can view questions submitted within their courses along with student names for pedagogical support, but student email addresses are protected.
5. **Transparency**: All AI-assisted features are explicitly labeled (e.g. "AI-generated voice", "AI teaching assistant").
